What is “Lis n Bliss”?

Lis n Bliss is a fun and easy listening app that helps users improve their English listening skills through different kinds of exercises.

How? The easiest way to understand is to jump in and play!

The app is designed based on important cognitive processes in language learning. Many learners find listening difficult because they need to hear, remember, and understand information at the same time. According to Baddeley’s Working Memory Model, the brain can become overloaded when processing too much sound information at once. Learners may also struggle with processing speed, attention control, and predicting meaning from context, which can make it hard to follow real-time speech.

To support these challenges, Lis n Bliss provides simple, interactive, and enjoyable activities that help users focus better, build listening confidence, and improve understanding step by step in a friendly learning environment.
